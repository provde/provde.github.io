ABOUT PROVDE

Clearer information.
Better-informed decisions.

AI helps people turn information into something new. We believe it should be easier to see what changed along the way.

WHY WE'RE BUILDING THIS

Good writing should keep the important details.

A rewritten document can be easier to read and still leave out an important condition. A summary can sound convincing and say more than its source supports.

As these versions get shared, the original context gets harder to find. The next person may not know there was anything to check.

We're building ProvDe to bring the change, the source, and the review together—before the wording becomes someone else's starting point.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Make the important details easier to see.

  1. Keep context within reach.

    People should be able to read the supporting text alongside the statement they are reviewing.

  2. Explain what needs attention.

    Point to the specific difference and the supporting text.

  3. Fit the way teams work.

    Bring review into the team's existing workflow.
